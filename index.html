08 / FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Who can attend?

The event is strictly for guests aged 18 and above.

How do I get my ticket?

Register with your name and WhatsApp number, complete the UPI payment, and wait for manual payment verification. Your ticket will be sent to your registered WhatsApp number after verification.

Is payment automatically verified?

No. Payment verification is manual. Registration details are submitted first, and the event team verifies the payment before issuing the ticket.

Will I be added to WhatsApp?

Confirmed attendees will be added to the official WhatsApp group after payment verification, within 24 hours.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellation requests must be sent personally to the event team within 24 hours of registration/payment. After 24 hours, no cancellation or refund will be accepted. No refund will be provided for non-attendance.

Can I take photographs and videos?

Guests are welcome to capture photos and videos using their personal phones. No submission is required.

Is the lantern finale confirmed?

1,000 sky lanterns are planned for the midnight finale, subject to final safety and regulatory approval. If approval is not confirmed, the experience will be referred to as the MIDNIGHT LIGHT FINALE.
